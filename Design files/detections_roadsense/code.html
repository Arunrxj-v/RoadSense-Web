Survey Methodology & Spatial Coverage

Observations are automatically recorded by transit-mounted optical sensors and calibrated accelerometer arrays. Detection confidence thresholds are established through dual-pass visual verification. Pavement sectors not registered in the chronological index represent segments awaiting active vehicle sweeps rather than certified defect-free conditions.

RoadSense displays automated road condition observations. Roads without markers may not have been recently surveyed.
